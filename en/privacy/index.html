Privacy Policy

FocusDensya is designed for private, personal focus journeys. It has no advertising, analytics, sign-up, or cross-app tracking. If you use the optional Invite campaign, the app exchanges a small amount of data with an invite service operated by the developer.

Provider: Shifeng ZhangEffective: November 12, 2026Support

1. Who provides FocusDensya

FocusDensya is provided by Shifeng Zhang. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to f.shera.09@gmail.com.

2. Data FocusDensya processes

Data that may sync through your private iCloud account

  • Task notes and reminder titles you enter.
  • Focus session and journey history, including duration and completion status.
  • Selected routes and stations, stamp progress, and collection targets.
  • Identifiers generated by the app to keep related records consistent.

This data is stored in the app’s private CloudKit database associated with your Apple ID. FocusDensya does not offer sign-up or a separate account system, and this data is not sent to a developer-controlled server.

Data processed by the invite service (only if you use the Invite campaign)

With the Invite campaign, you can share an invite ticket. A friend who enters your invite code receives a 7-day trial commuter pass, and you receive a monthly, yearly, or lifetime commuter pass depending on how many invited friends count. To run the campaign, the app contacts an invite service that the developer operates on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1 (focusdensya-invite.podnote-api.workers.dev). The app contacts it only after you open the Invite screen.

  • Sent with every request: the App Store–signed AppTransaction for your copy of FocusDensya. The service verifies Apple’s signature, uses it to recognize the same Apple Account and prevent duplicate redemptions, and checks your original download date to confirm that you can be invited. The AppTransaction itself is not stored.
  • User ID: the appTransactionID that Apple assigns to your Apple Account’s download of FocusDensya, stored as your user ID.
  • Invite code: your invite code and when it was issued.
  • Redemptions: when a code is used, the user IDs of the inviter and the invited friend, the code, the time it was redeemed, and the time the invite started to count.
  • Arrival dates (invited friends only): the calendar dates (YYYY-MM-DD) on which you arrived at a station, sent only until your invite counts. Routes, stations, tasks, and other focus details are not sent.

The invite service does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and it does not store IP addresses or device information in its database. Inviters see only how many invites are pending or counted, not who redeemed their code. Data from the App Store production environment and from the Sandbox environment (TestFlight and App Review) is stored separately.

Scanning an invite QR code or opening an invite link loads an invite page (GET /i/<code>) from the same service. The page shows the invite code and a link to the App Store, and chooses its language from your browser’s language setting. It uses no cookies or analytics, and the service does not store anything about the visit.

Data processed only on your device or in the App Group

  • The apps and categories you select with Apple Screen Time.
  • Notification preferences.
  • Current Live Activity and journey handoff state.

FocusDensya does not upload your Screen Time selection to the developer.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to provide core FocusDensya features, synchronize your progress between your Apple devices, and restore your data. Invite service data is used only to run the Invite campaign: issuing invite codes, applying trial passes, counting invites, granting rewards, and preventing duplicate or reciprocal redemptions. No data is used for advertising, marketing, third-party analytics, profiling, or tracking.

4. Sharing and sale

FocusDensya does not sell personal data and does not share it with advertising or analytics companies. If you enable iCloud, Apple processes synced data as the CloudKit service provider under Apple’s terms and privacy commitments. If you use the Invite campaign, Cloudflare hosts the invite service and processes its requests, including the IP address needed to deliver them, as the infrastructure provider under Cloudflare’s terms and privacy commitments. Disclosure may occur only when required by applicable law.

5. Permissions

Notifications, Screen Time app shielding, Live Activities, and iCloud are optional. Denying a permission does not prevent you from using the core focus timer. Screen Time authorization is used only to shield apps you choose while an active focus journey is running; it is not used for parental monitoring.

6. Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device and, when iCloud is enabled, in your private iCloud database until you delete them. You can remove individual reminders and records in the app where deletion is available. To remove local app data, delete FocusDensya from your device. To remove synchronized data, use your device’s iCloud storage management for FocusDensya; labels may vary by iOS version. You can also contact support for guidance.

Invite service records are kept on the service and are not deleted automatically. Deleting the app or its iCloud data does not remove them. To have them deleted, email f.shera.09@gmail.com with the invite code shown on your Invite screen. Deleting these records may end commuter passes and rewards that depend on them.

7. Security and international processing

FocusDensya uses Apple platform storage and private CloudKit synchronization. The app communicates with the invite service over HTTPS, and the service stores its records in Cloudflare D1. No method of storage is completely risk-free. Apple may process iCloud data, and Cloudflare may process invite service data, in locations described in their own privacy documentation.

8. Children

FocusDensya is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data for advertising and does not require you to sign up.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version and effective date will be posted on this page.
